## Long Description (Max 4,000 characters)

Whether you need to scan a website link, connect instantly to secure Wi-Fi, share business contact cards, or generate custom QR codes for your brand, **ScanKit** provides all the essential tools in one lightning-fast application.

### Key Features
- **Lightning-Fast Live Scanner**: Instantly detect and decode QR codes, barcodes, and matrix codes with precision camera autofocus, zoom, and flashlight support.
- **Gallery Image Scanning**: Select screenshots or saved photos directly from your device to scan QR codes effortlessly.
- **Versatile QR Studio Generator**: Create custom QR codes for Website URLs, Wi-Fi networks, vCard Contacts, Email, Phone Numbers, Social Links (Instagram, X/Twitter, LinkedIn, GitHub, YouTube, TikTok, WhatsApp), and Plain Text.
- **Professional Customization**: Style your QR codes with precision foreground color palettes and rounded or sharp module designs.
- **Smart Contextual Actions**: One-tap website opening, direct dialer, Wi-Fi settings configuration with password copy, and vCard contact saving.
- **Persistent History**: Automatically save, search, favorite, copy, and organize all your scanned and created records.
- **High-Res Export & Sharing**: Save crisp (1024×1024) PNG images directly to your gallery and share QR codes seamlessly.
- **Clean Material 3 Interface**: Designed with modern Material Design 3 guidelines, edge-to-edge support, adaptive tablet layouts, and custom typography.

Create, scan, save, and share QR codes wherever you are with ScanKit!
